Automation Bot Uptime Tracker

Paste your status check log (one entry per line, format: timestamp,status) and get an uptime summary.

Where This Log Comes From

You can generate this format from your own monitoring script, a cron job that pings your bot's health endpoint, or by exporting logs from a monitoring service and reformatting them to timestamp,status per line.

Client-side tool. Nothing you paste here is stored or sent anywhere.
